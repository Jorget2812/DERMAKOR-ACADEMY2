# Pricing Rules: DERMAKOR ACADEMY

## 1. Professional Level: NONE
- **Access**: Shop (Verified), NO Academy.
- **VAT**: Included (8.1%).
- **Discount**: 0% (Base Price).
- **Example**:
  - Base Price: 100.00 CHF
  - Discount: 0%
  - Net: 92.51 CHF
  - VAT (8.1%): 7.49 CHF
  - **Gross (Final)**: 100.00 CHF (Invoice shows "VAT Included")

## 2. Professional Level: STANDARD
- **Access**: Shop (Verified), Academy (Standard content).
- **VAT**: Not included (0% logic).
- **Discount**: Category Override or Monthly (Max 30%).
- **Example**:
  - Base Price: 100.00 CHF
  - Discount (30%): -30.00 CHF
  - Net: 70.00 CHF
  - VAT (0%): 0.00 CHF
  - **Gross (Final)**: 70.00 CHF

## 3. Professional Level: PREMIUM

- **Discount**: Category Override or Monthly (Max 50%).
- **Example**:

  - Discount (50%): -50.00 CHF

  - VAT (0%): 0.00 CHF
  - **Gross (Final)**: 50.00 CHF
